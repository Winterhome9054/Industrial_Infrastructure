# Industrial Infrastructure

An attempt at integrating industrial scale crafting equipment and a new lategame progression tied not to technology advancement, but rather to scale.

## Current features:
- Industrial Alkaline Electrolyzer, a heavy duty piece of equipment using potassium hydroxide as a nonconsumable electrolyte alongside high energy input to convert clean water into bulk hydrogen gas.
- Asbestos. It's in some residential walls, and it's in the insulation around water heaters.
- An alternate pathway to production of potassium hydroxide, using slaked lime, pearlash, and water, with a byproduct of calcium carbonate that can be re-calcined into quicklime.
- Hydrogen Gas as an environmental hazard. Don't make more than you can contain in the presence of open flame.

## Currently Planned Features:
- Oxygen displacement asphyxiation.
- Plastic precursors chemicals.
- Structural Plastic (HDPE), Armor Grade Plastic (UHMWPE), relevant components.
- Chemical supply company map special.
- Industrial scale plastic forming equipment.
- Industrial theme mobs.
